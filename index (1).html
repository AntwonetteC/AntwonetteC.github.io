About This Trip

Experience the Wonder

Join Vacation With Grace Travel & Tours for an unforgettable faith-based group getaway to the Ark Encounter and Creation Museum in Kentucky. Travel in comfort on a luxury motor coach with everything handled for you — all you have to do is show up and enjoy!

⛵

Ark Encounter

Walk through the life-size Noah's Ark — one of the most awe-inspiring faith-based attractions in the world. Seven decks of exhibits bringing the Bible to life.

🌍

Creation Museum

Explore 75,000 square feet of world-class exhibits presenting a biblical worldview of history and science in a stunning, immersive setting.

🚌

Luxury Motor Coach

Sit back and relax on a deluxe motor coach. No driving, no stress — just fellowship and great company all the way there and back.

🏨

Hotel Accommodations

Comfortable, quality hotel accommodations included for all 4 nights. Rest easy knowing everything is taken care of.

Trip Itinerary

Your 5-Day Journey

Every detail carefully planned so you can focus on the experience.

Day 1 · October 30

Departure Day — Let the Adventure Begin!

Board the luxury motor coach and head out with your group. Get settled into your hotel, enjoy dinner, and get ready for an amazing journey ahead.

Day 2 · October 31

Ark Encounter

Spend the full day exploring the life-size Noah's Ark. Seven decks of breathtaking exhibits, live animals, and inspiring biblical presentations. A truly once-in-a-lifetime experience.

Day 3 · November 1

Creation Museum

Discover the wonders of creation at this world-class museum. Walk through stunning dioramas, planetarium shows, and beautifully landscaped botanical gardens.

Day 4 · November 2

Free Time & Fellowship

Enjoy leisure time for shopping, exploring local attractions, or simply relaxing with your fellow travelers. A perfect day to soak it all in.

Day 5 · November 3

Homeward Bound

After breakfast, board the motor coach for the return trip home — hearts full, memories made, and spirits renewed.
