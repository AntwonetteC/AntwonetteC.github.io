Day by Day

Your 5-Day Journey

Every detail is planned so all you have to do is show up, relax, and enjoy the experience.

Day 1 · October 30, 2026

Departure Day — The Adventure Begins!

Board the luxury motor coach and head out with your group. Fellowship starts before you even arrive! Get settled into your hotel, enjoy a group dinner, and get excited for what's ahead.

Day 2 · October 31, 2026

Ark Encounter

Walk through the breathtaking life-size Noah's Ark — all 510 feet of it! Seven decks of stunning exhibits, live animals, and biblical history brought to life. A truly once-in-a-lifetime experience you'll never forget.

Day 3 · November 1, 2026

Creation Museum

Explore 75,000 square feet of world-class exhibits at the Creation Museum. Walk through stunning dioramas, botanical gardens, a planetarium show, and so much more. Faith and science beautifully woven together.

Day 4 · November 2, 2026

BB Riverboat Cruise & Free Time

Enjoy a scenic BB Riverboat Cruise along the Ohio River — a beautiful, relaxing experience. Afternoon free time for shopping, exploring, or simply soaking in the fellowship with your travel companions.

Day 5 · November 3, 2026

Homeward Bound

After breakfast, board the motor coach for the journey home. Hearts full, spirits renewed, and memories made that will last a lifetime. More than a trip — it's an experience!

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you book.

What is included in the $775 trip price? +
Your $775 per person covers everything listed: round-trip deluxe motor coach transportation, 4 nights hotel accommodations, 4 breakfasts & 4 dinners, snacks on the bus, admission to the Ark Encounter, Creation Museum & BB Riverboat Cruise, on-board raffles/games/prizes, and all gratuities for included services.
What is NOT included in the trip price? +
Items not included are: travel insurance (available — ask Antwonette!), personal spending money, souvenirs, meals outside of the included breakfasts and dinners, and any personal items or incidentals during free time.
How does the $75 deposit work? +
A $75 per person non-refundable deposit is required to reserve your spot. This deposit is applied directly toward your total trip cost of $775 — it is not an extra charge. Your remaining balance will be due according to the payment schedule Antwonette provides.
What does "double occupancy" mean? +
Double occupancy means the $775 price is based on two people sharing a hotel room. If you wish to have your own room (single occupancy), a single supplement fee will apply. Contact Antwonette for single occupancy pricing.
Is travel insurance required? +
Travel insurance is not required but is strongly recommended. A travel protection plan can cover unexpected cancellations, medical emergencies, trip interruptions, lost luggage, and more. Ask Antwonette about available options when she contacts you.
What if I need to cancel? +
The $75 deposit is non-refundable. Full cancellation terms and deadlines will be provided in your booking documents. This is another reason travel insurance is highly recommended — it can protect your investment in the event of an unexpected cancellation.
What should I bring? +
Pack comfortable walking shoes, weather-appropriate clothing for late October/early November in Kentucky (it can be cool!), a valid government-issued photo ID, a camera to capture all the memories, and spending money for souvenirs and personal purchases during free time.
How do I secure my spot? +
Simply fill out the interest form on this page! Antwonette will contact you personally to walk you through the booking process, payment options, and next steps. Spots are limited so don't wait!
