Day by Day

Your 5-Day Journey

Every detail is planned so all you have to do is show up, relax, and enjoy the experience.

Day 1 · October 30 — Departure Day

The Adventure Begins!

Depart early morning from Stone Mountain, Georgia aboard a deluxe motor coach. Sit back, relax, and enjoy the journey. Upon arrival, check in to your hotel and enjoy dinner before settling in for the evening.

✦ Meals Included: Dinner

Day 2 · October 31 — Ark Encounter

Walk Through Noah's Ark

After breakfast, depart for the world-famous Ark Encounter. Experience the life-size reconstruction of Noah's Ark and explore exhibits that bring biblical history to life across seven breathtaking decks.

✦ Meals Included: Breakfast, Dinner

Day 3 · November 1 — Creation Museum

Faith & Science Come Alive

Enjoy breakfast before departing for the Creation Museum. Explore biblical history exhibits, beautiful botanical gardens, a planetarium show, and educational experiences designed for visitors of all ages.

✦ Meals Included: Breakfast, Dinner

Day 4 · November 2 — Freedom Center & River Cruise

History & Scenic Beauty

After breakfast, visit the National Underground Railroad Freedom Center — a powerful, moving experience honoring the journey to freedom. Later, enjoy a BB Riverboat Sightseeing Cruise aboard a fully enclosed, temperature-controlled vessel on the scenic Ohio River. Enjoy dinner before returning to the hotel for your final evening.

✦ Meals Included: Breakfast, Dinner

Day 5 · November 3 — Return Home

Homeward Bound

Enjoy breakfast before checking out of the hotel and boarding the motor coach for the journey back to Stone Mountain, Georgia. Hearts full, spirits renewed, memories made that will last a lifetime.

✦ Meals Included: Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the $75 deposit refundable?+
No. The $75 deposit is non-refundable and is applied toward the total trip cost of $775.
Is travel insurance included?+
No. Travel insurance is not included but is highly recommended. Cancel For Any Reason and Interrupt For Any Reason coverage may be available as an optional add-on when the full trip cost is insured within 14 days of the initial trip deposit. Ask Antwonette for details.
Is single occupancy available?+
Single occupancy may be available at an additional cost and is based on availability. Contact Antwonette for pricing.
Do you offer roommate matching?+
No. Roommate matching is not available. Travelers requesting double occupancy must provide their own roommate.
When is final payment due?+
Final payment is due September 5, 2026. Monthly payment options are available. Antwonette will provide your payment schedule upon booking.
Are seats limited?+
Yes. Seats are limited and available on a first-come, first-served basis. Your seat is only secured upon receipt of your $75 non-refundable deposit.
How do I reserve my seat?+
Complete the request form below. Once your information is received, Vacation With Grace Travel and Tours will send you booking instructions and deposit information. Submitting the form does not guarantee your reservation until your deposit is received.
What should I bring?+
Pack comfortable walking shoes, weather-appropriate clothing for late October/early November in Kentucky (it can be cool!), a valid government-issued photo ID, spending money for lunches, souvenirs, and personal purchases, and a camera to capture all the memories!
